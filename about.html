Understanding Kinesiology

Kinesiology is a non-invasive holistic therapy that uses Bio-feedback—specifically muscle monitoring—to identify imbalances in the body's structural, chemical, and emotional systems.

By assessing the "on/off" response of specific muscles, Catherine can bypass the conscious mind to communicate directly with the nervous system. This allows her to pinpoint the root causes of fatigue, pain, or emotional blockages that traditional methods might overlook.
